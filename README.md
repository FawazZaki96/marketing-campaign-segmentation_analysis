# 📊 Marketing Campaign Optimization: Segment-Based Targeting Strategy

## 🧠 Business Problem

Marketing campaigns often target broad customer groups without understanding which segments deliver the highest return.

This project aims to answer:

> **Which customer segments should the business prioritize to maximize campaign effectiveness and revenue impact?**

---

## 🎯 Objectives

* Identify high-performing customer segments
* Compare **response rate vs customer size vs spending value**
* Evaluate **marketing efficiency across segments**
* Provide **data-driven targeting recommendations**

---

## 🗂️ Dataset

* Source: Marketing Campaign Dataset
* Total Customers: **2,240**
* Key Features:

  * Customer income
  * Purchase behavior (spending)
  * Campaign response (0/1)

---

## 📊 Key Metrics

* Total Customers: **2,240**
* Response Rate: **14.91%**
* Total Spending: **1.36M**

---

## 🔍 Key Insights

### 1. High-Spending Customers Drive Campaign Effectiveness

* Response rate: **~42%**
* 3–4x higher than low-spending segments

---

### 2. Spending Behavior Outperforms Income as Predictor

* High-income ≠ high response
* **High spending is the strongest indicator of engagement**

---

### 3. Trade-Off Identified: Size vs Efficiency

* **Medium–Low segment**

  * Largest customer base (~865 customers)
  * Low response rate (~10%)
  * Inefficient for broad campaigns

---

### 4. Revenue vs Response Trade-Off

* Medium spenders contribute the **highest total revenue (~0.73M)**
* High spenders deliver the **highest response rates**

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations

* **Prioritize high-spending customers** for marketing campaigns
* **Reallocate budget** toward high-response segments
* Use **low-cost channels (email, targeted offers)** for low-performing segments
* Avoid broad campaigns for large but inefficient segments
